Our Story

Born From a Love
of Ancient Scent

Firdhousi Fragrances began with a simple belief: that a true attar should be felt, not just smelled — rich, long-lasting, and free from alcohol, the way perfumery was practiced for centuries across Arabia and the subcontinent.

Every bottle we blend draws on that tradition — oud, rose, saffron, musk and amber, sourced with care and matured patiently before it ever reaches you.

— The Firdhousi House
